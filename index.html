About VocoLoco

VocoLoco is a free text-to-speech (TTS) studio that runs entirely in your browser. Type or paste your text (up to 2000 characters), pick or clone a voice, then listen or download an MP3. Speech is generated on your own device with the OmniVoice model on WebGPU, so your text and recordings are never uploaded. It speaks 600+ languages, clones a voice from a short recording (transcribed automatically on your device), and runs on desktop (Chrome, Edge or Safari 26; other browsers fall back to a much slower CPU mode) and on phones and tablets (iOS or iPadOS 26 in any browser, Chrome 121 or newer on Android).

The first visit downloads the model from Hugging Face: about 1.2 GB, or about 2.7 GB with voice cloning. Once it is stored on your device, VocoLoco works offline. The app is open source on GitHub (Apache-2.0); the models are for non-commercial use (licences under Disclaimer & privacy).

  • Pause buttons: [pause] and [pause 2s], or type [pause 1.5] for an exact silence

Disclaimer & Legal Notice

How it works. All speech synthesis and transcription runs locally in your browser. No audio, text, or voice data is uploaded to any server. However, on first use (and after cache clears), the app downloads AI model weights (~1.2 GB, plus ~1.5 GB for voice cloning and automatic transcription) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co); browsers without WebGPU also load a runtime library from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). These downloads expose your IP address, browser user-agent, and referrer to those services under their respective privacy policies. Once cached, no further network requests are made during synthesis.
